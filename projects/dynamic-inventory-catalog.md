## The Challenge

The sales team was juggling product information scattered across multiple vendor catalogs: PDFs, spreadsheets, outdated printouts, the usual mess. Finding accurate specs mid-call meant fumbling through documents, and customers had no way to browse offerings on their own. To make it worse, the business only carried a subset of each vendor's products, and there was no clean way to filter down to just what they actually sold.

## Our Approach

We built a centralized product catalog system that consolidates data from multiple vendor sources into a single, searchable interface. The admin backend lets staff import vendor catalogs and selectively include only the products they carry, filtering out everything else. The frontend serves double duty: an internal tool for the sales team and segmented customer-facing views for self-service browsing.

## Key Features

- **Vendor Catalog Consolidation**: Import and merge product data from multiple vendors into one unified catalog, so nobody has to reference five different sources
- **Selective Product Curation**: Filter out products the business doesn't carry, so the catalog only shows what's actually available for sale
- **Internal Sales Tool**: Staff quickly search and pull accurate product specs, images, and pricing during sales calls
- **Customer-Facing Segments**: Filtered catalog views let customers browse offerings independently, cutting down pre-sales support requests

## The Results

- Gave the sales team instant access to accurate product information during calls
- Let customers self-serve product research, reducing inbound inquiries
- Consolidated five-plus vendor catalogs into a single source of truth
- Streamlined onboarding of new products by filtering right at import

## Technologies Used

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, local hosting
