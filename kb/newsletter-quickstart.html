Newsletter Platform — Quick Start Guide

Your newsletter platform is powered by Keila, a modern email marketing tool. This guide will get you sending your first campaign in under 15 minutes.

1. Logging In

  • URL: https://mail.serendipitylabs.cloud
  • Email: (provided during onboarding)
  • Password: You set this via the welcome email's password reset link

Bookmark this URL — you'll use it every time you send a newsletter.

Forgot your password? Click "Reset password" on the login page. If that doesn't work, contact us and we'll send a new reset link.

2. Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your project — this is your dedicated workspace. Everything you do (contacts, campaigns, templates) lives inside your project.

From the project dashboard, you can access:

  • Contacts — your subscriber list
  • Campaigns — create and send newsletters
  • Templates — reusable email designs
  • Forms — signup form settings (we manage these for you)

3. Your Contacts

How subscribers get added

  • Automatically — anyone who signs up through your website's newsletter form is added to your contact list in real time. You don't need to do anything.
  • Manually — you can add individual contacts from the Contacts page (click "Create Contact").
  • Import — have an existing list? Click "Import" on the Contacts page and upload a CSV file with at minimum an email column.

Managing contacts

  • Click any contact to see their details and activity
  • Contacts who unsubscribe are automatically removed from future sends
  • You can delete contacts manually if needed

Segments (optional, for targeted sends)

If you want to send to a subset of your list (e.g., only people in a certain zip code, or people who signed up after a certain date):

  1. Go to Contacts → Segments
  2. Click Create Segment
  3. Add filter rules (e.g., "signed up after March 1" or custom fields)
  4. Save — you can select this segment when sending a campaign

4. Creating a Campaign

We've pre-loaded your account with ready-to-use campaign designs that match your branding. To send a newsletter, you'll clone one of these and customize the content.

Step 1: Clone a campaign

  1. Go to Campaigns
  2. Find one of the pre-loaded campaigns (labeled by type — e.g., "Newsletter," "Announcement," "Event")
  3. Click Clone to create a copy
  4. Give it a new subject line (this is what recipients see in their inbox)

Step 2: Customize the content

The cloned campaign already has your branding, layout, and structure. Just update:

  • Text — swap in your actual message
  • Images — click any image block to replace it (keep images under 1MB each)
  • Links/buttons — update URLs to point to the right pages
  • Preview — always click "Preview" before sending to see how it looks

Tip: Don't rearrange the layout — the pre-loaded designs are optimized for email clients. Just change the words and images.

Step 3: Select recipients

  • Choose "All contacts" or a specific segment if you've set one up

Step 4: Send or schedule

  • Send now — delivers immediately to all selected recipients
  • Schedule — pick a future date and time; the system sends it automatically

5. After You Send

Campaign stats

After sending, click on the campaign to see:

  • Delivered — how many emails reached inboxes
  • Opened — how many people opened your email (note: Apple Mail privacy features can inflate this)
  • Clicked — how many people clicked a link
  • Unsubscribed — how many opted out (some is normal — typically under 1%)

Focus on trends over time rather than any single send — what matters is that your audience stays engaged.

6. Common Tasks

"I want to resend to people who didn't open"

  1. Go to the sent campaign
  2. Create a new campaign with a tweaked subject line
  3. Use a segment: "Did not open [campaign name]" (if available) or contact us and we'll set it up

"I want to add a signup form to a page that doesn't have one"

Contact us — we'll add the form and wire it up. Takes about 15 minutes on our end.

"I want to change my logo or colors in the email template"

Contact us — we'll update your template to match any branding changes. Or if you're comfortable in the Block Editor, you can swap images and colors directly.

"I accidentally sent to the wrong list"

Unfortunately, emails can't be recalled once sent. Double-check your recipient selection on the send screen. When in doubt, send a test first (see below).

"How do I send a test email to myself?"

In the campaign editor, click "Send & Schedule" then click "Send Preview" — enter your own email address to see exactly what subscribers will receive.

7. Best Practices

  1. Be consistent — pick a cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly) and stick to it. Irregular sends train people to ignore you.
  2. Respect the unsubscribe — it's legally required (CAN-SPAM) and handled automatically. Never re-add someone who unsubscribed.
  3. Don't buy email lists — ever. It tanks your deliverability and can get your sending domain blacklisted.
  4. Write for humans — conversational > corporate. People open emails from people, not organizations.
  5. One CTA per email — what's the ONE thing you want the reader to do? Make it obvious.
  6. Mobile matters — 60%+ of emails are read on phones. Our templates are mobile-responsive, but keep paragraphs short and buttons large.
